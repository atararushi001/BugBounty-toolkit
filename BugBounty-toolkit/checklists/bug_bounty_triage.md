# Bug Bounty Triage Checklist

For every suspected issue:

1. Confirm the affected asset is in scope.
2. Record the exact URL/request.
3. Identify the affected parameter/object.
4. Establish a minimal safe reproduction.
5. Determine whether authentication is required.
6. Determine impact.
7. Check whether the behavior is already documented/expected.
8. Preserve request/response evidence.
9. Avoid destructive or privacy-impacting actions.
10. Write a concise reproduction and impact statement.
